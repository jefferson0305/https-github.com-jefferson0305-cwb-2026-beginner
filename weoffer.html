TOMIRO'S JUICE SHOP

History

One sunny afternoon, two best friends, Tomo and Miro, decided to open a small juice shop.

Tomo loved making fruit juices, while Miro was good at talking to customers. They started working together to make their dream come true.

At first, only a few people came to the shop. But the friends worked hard and always welcomed their customers.

One evening, Tomo said, "I think our little shop is becoming something special."

Miro smiled and replied, "It's because we built it together."

From that day on, their juice shop became a favorite place in the neighborhood, known for fresh juice and friendly service.

Employees

  • Tomo - Juice Maker: Prepares fresh and delicious fruit juices.
  • Miro - Customer Service: Welcomes customers and takes orders.
  • Shop Assistants: Help keep the shop clean and serve customers.
